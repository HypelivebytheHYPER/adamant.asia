## 1. Brand Philosophy: What Adamant Means

### The Name
**Adamant** — from the Greek *adamas*, "unbreakable." Not stubborn. Not rigid. Something that cannot be worn down. A diamond is adamant. So is a person who refuses to let their vision dissolve into busywork.

### Core Belief
> The best ideas do not come from the biggest teams. They come from the most determined minds.

We do not sell AI. We sell **momentum** — the feeling that your work is moving forward even when you are not pushing it.

### What We Reject
| Cliché | Why We Reject It |
|--------|-----------------|
| "AI for SMEs" | Too generic. Says nothing. |
| "Democratizing AI" | NGO language. Not our fight. |
| "Future of work" | Vague. Everyone claims the future. |
| "One-person unicorn" | Cringe. Trivializes real struggle. |
| "10x your productivity" | Snake oil. We do not promise multiples. |
| "Unlock your potential" | Key metaphor is exhausted. |

### What We Stand For
| Principle | Expression |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Workflow architects** | We design systems, not just tools. |
| **For the determined** | Our customer is someone who will not quit. |
| **Invisible infrastructure** | Best compliment: "I forgot AI was doing that." |
| **Southeast Asian by default** | LINE, Facebook, Thai tax rules — native. |

## 2. Voice & Tone: How Adamant Speaks

### The Five Voice Rules

| Rule | Do | Don't |
|------|-----|-------|
| **1. Confident, not arrogant** | "We build it. You use it." | "We are the best AI agency in SEA." |
| **2. Warm, not casual** | "Your workflow" | "Your flow, fam 🔥" |
| **3. Precise, not robotic** | Every word earns its place. | Filler, jargon, buzzwords. |
| **4. Determined, not desperate** | We know who we are. | "Limited time offer!" |
| **5. Understated, not boring** | Specific over superlative. | "Revolutionary game-changer." |

### Forbidden Words (Permanent Ban)
- "Revolutionary" — unless you are literally overthrowing a government
- "Game-changer" — dead since 2015
- "Leverage" (as verb) — corporate poison
- "Synergy" — banned forever
- "Disrupt" — unless you are breaking something physical
- "Empower" — patronizing
- "Unlock" — key metaphor is exhausted
- "Democratize" — NGO language
- "Supercharge" — car metaphor, not ours
- "Transform" — overused to meaninglessness
- "Innovate" — say what you actually do
- "Scalable" — enterprise speak
- "Seamless" — nothing is seamless
- "Frictionless" — friction is sometimes good
- "Holistic" — vague
- "Ecosystem" — unless you are biologist
- "Journey" — customer journey is dead
- "Experience" — too broad
- "Solution" / "solutions" — say the thing, not the category. Never use even when describing what others sell.
- "Platform" — say what it does. Never use as a euphemism for "solution."

### Preferred Language
| Weak | Strong |
|------|--------|
| "Create" | "Build" (implies craft) |
| "Develop" | "Design" (implies intention) |
| "Execute" | "Run" (implies continuity) |
| "Our users" | "You" (direct, personal) |
| "Leverage AI" | "Use AI to..." (specific) |
| "Optimize workflows" | "Cut the steps that waste your time" |
| "Enhance productivity" | "Get the boring stuff done faster" |
| "Streamline processes" | "Remove the friction" |

### Tone by Context
| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| Hero headline | Bold, editorial, slightly poetic | "Big vision *doesn't* need a big company." |
| Body copy | Clear, grounded, specific | "We map how you work. Then we build a system that fits." |
| CTA buttons | Direct, action-oriented | "Start a project" — not "Request a demo" |
| Error states | Helpful, human, never blaming | "Something went wrong. We are on it." |
| Social proof | Factual, understated | "12 hours back in her week." — Name, Role |
| Pricing | Transparent, no games | "฿X,XXX/month. No setup fee." |

## 3. Messaging Framework: The Adamant Story

### The Story Arc
1. **Hook** — Acknowledge the gap between ambition and tools.
2. **Problem** — Enterprise AI ignores people like you.
3. **Belief** — Determination matters more than headcount.
4. **Process** — How we turn your vision into a workflow.
5. **Proof** — What we build (not what we promise).
6. **Conversion** — Start the conversation.

### Headline Patterns

| Pattern | How It Works | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **Roman + Italic** | Serif authority + human emphasis | "Big vision *doesn't* need a big company." |
| **Negative framing** | Define by what you are not | "Not enterprise software. Your software." |
| **Determination** | Appeal to will, not size | "Built for those who will not quit." |
| **Invisibility** | Best tool is the one you forget | "You will forget it is there." |
| **Specific outcome** | Numbers over adjectives | "12 hours back in your week." |
| **Direct address** | Speak to one person | "Your workflow, *amplified.*" |

### Social Proof Style
- ❌ No star ratings
- ❌ No "trusted by 10,000+ companies"
- ❌ No logos without context
- ✅ Specific outcomes
- ✅ Named people
- ✅ Real numbers

> "We saved 12 hours a week on client reporting."
> — Thida, Operations Lead, Bangkok Design Studio

## 4. Visual Identity

### Color Philosophy
Warm editorial — not cold tech, not neon startup.

| Token | Hex | Meaning |
|-------|-----|---------|
| **Paper** | `#F8F7F4` | Warmth, breath, editorial canvas |
| **Ink** | `#1A1A19` | Authority, depth (not pure black) |
| **Teal** | `#0F766E` | SEA ocean, trustworthy AI |
| **Amber** | `#B45309` | Human warmth, sunset optimism |
| **Mist** | `#EFEDEA` | Subtle separation |
| **Stone** | `#6B6B67` | Supporting text |

### Typography
| Role | Font | Weight |
|------|------|--------|
| **Display / Hero** | Newsreader | 400 (Regular) |
| **UI / Body** | Geist Sans | 400–600 |
| **Code / Data** | Geist Mono | 400–500 |

**Hero pattern**: Roman + Italic in same line.

### Spacing
- Sections: 80–128px vertical padding
- Reading width: 720px max
- Layout width: 1400px max

### Shape
- Radius: 14px base
- Buttons: Pill shape
- Cards: 14–18px radius

## 5. Motion & Animation

### Philosophy
Motion feels **inevitable**, not performative. Like a door that swings open because someone pushed it.

### Easing
| Name | Curve | Use |
|------|-------|-----|
| **Spring** | `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)` | Hover, card lift |
| **Smooth** | `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` | Standard transitions |
| **Dramatic** | `cubic-bezier(0.87, 0, 0.13, 1)` | Hero entrances |

### Avoid
- Parallax that fights scroll
- Cursor followers
- Bouncy entrances on serious content

## 6. Layout Principles

### Asymmetric Editorial
- Text anchors left
- Visuals float right or bleed full-width
- Never center everything

### Content Density
- **Hero**: Sparse. Headline, subhead, two CTAs.
- **Body**: Moderate. One idea per section.
- **Cards**: Title + 2-line description max.

## 7. Component Language

### Buttons
| Variant | Style |
|---------|-------|
| **Primary** | Ink fill, white text, pill. Hover: Teal. |
| **Secondary** | Transparent, ink border. Hover: Mist fill. |
| **Text** | No container. Ink text + arrow. Hover: underline. |

### Cards
- White or Mist background
- 14px radius
- 1px border at 4% opacity
- Shadow on hover only
- No gradients

### Navigation
- Fixed, transparent over hero
- Glass effect on scroll
- Logo left, links center, CTA right

## 8. Photography & Imagery

### Direction
- ❌ No stock photos of people pointing at screens
- ❌ No AI-generated "business people"
- ✅ Natural textures, SEA landscapes, hands at work, morning light
- ✅ Warm, slightly desaturated, high contrast

### Abstract Elements
- Organic shapes in Teal at 8–12% opacity
- Fine line illustrations (single weight, ink color)
- No 3D renders, no isometric illustrations

## Appendix: Word Philosophy — Learning from the Greats

### Apple: The Power of Absence
> "Think Different." — Two words. No verb. No product. Pure attitude.

**Lesson**: The best messages say less. Apple's headlines never explain. They declare. "Shot on iPhone." "Privacy. That's iPhone." Every word is a choice to exclude.

**Adamant application**: "Your workflow, *amplified.*" — Not "We amplify your workflow with AI." The subject is you. The verb is implied. The italics do the work.

### Tesla: Provocation as Positioning
> "The quickest car in the world." — Not "one of." Not "among." The.

**Lesson**: Tesla speaks in absolutes. Not because they are always true, but because confidence creates reality. "Autopilot." "Ludicrous mode." The names are claims.

**Adamant application**: "Built for those who will not quit." — Not "for people who work hard." Will not quit is absolute. It is a line in the sand.

### Uber: The Question as Invitation
> "Where to?" — Two words. A question, not a statement. The entire product in a phrase.

**Lesson**: Uber's best copy is a conversation starter. Not "Ride with Uber." Not "Get a ride." Just "Where to?" It assumes you are already leaving.

**Adamant application**: "What are you building?" — On the contact page. Not "Contact us." Not "Get in touch." A question that assumes ambition.

### Visa: The Promise of Possibility
> "Everywhere you want to be." — Not " accepted everywhere." Not "use us anywhere." Everywhere *you want to be.*

**Lesson**: Visa does not sell payment processing. They sell aspiration. The card is a key to a life. The tagline is about the customer's desire, not the product's feature.

**Adamant application**: "Your vision is adamant. So are we." — Not "We build AI workflows." The customer's vision is the hero. We are the support.

### Ogilvy: The Discipline of Clarity
> "The consumer isn't a moron. She is your wife." — Respect the reader's intelligence.

> "You cannot bore people into buying your product. You can only interest them." — Every word must earn its place.

> "When you advertise fire extinguishers, open with the fire." — Lead with the problem, not the solution.

> "Never use jargon words like reconceptualize, demassification, attitudinally. They are hallmarks of a pretentious ass." — Plain speaking wins.

> "On average, five times as many people read the headline as read the body copy. When you have written your headline, you have spent eighty cents out of your dollar." — The headline is everything.

> "Tell the truth, but make the truth fascinating." — Honesty + craft.

**Lesson**: Ogilvy's entire philosophy is respect. Respect for the reader's time, intelligence, and attention. No tricks. No manipulation. Just clear, honest, interesting communication.

**Adamant application**: Every headline must pass the Ogilvy test: Would you say this to your spouse? Would they understand it immediately? Would they care?
